VioPTT: Violin Technique-Aware
Piano Transcription with Synthetic Data

Automatic transcription and per-note playing technique classification for violin

Yueh-Po Peng  ·  Academia Sinica

GitHub Audio Examples Results

Abstract

We present VioPTT, a system for automatic violin transcription with per-note playing technique classification. Given a raw violin audio recording, VioPTT outputs a MIDI transcription and labels each note with one of four playing techniques: flageolet, normal, pizzicato, and spiccato.

Our approach leverages synthetic data augmentation to bridge the gap between limited annotated data and the diversity of real-world violin recordings. A pretrained piano transcription model provides intermediate representations (onset, offset, frame, and velocity activations) that serve as rich acoustic features for technique classification.
